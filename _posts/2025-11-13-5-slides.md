## 1. Zeile der Argumentationsanalyse

> Argumentation is a verbal, social, and rational activity aimed at convincing a reasonable critic of the acceptability of a standpoint by putting forward a constellation of propositions justifying or refuting the proposition expressed in the standpoint. (Eemeren & Grootendorst 2003, 1)
